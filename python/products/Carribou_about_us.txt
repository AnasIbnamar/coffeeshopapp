At Carribou Coffee Shop, we believe every cup tells a story—yours, ours, and the coffee's journey from bean to brew. Nestled in the heart of Rabat, Carribou is more than just a coffee shop; it's a cozy escape where flavors meet friendship and passion brews every moment.

Our Story
Carribou was founded by Anas Ibnamar and Mohamed Amine Zoumhan, two lifelong friends with a shared dream of bringing the world's finest coffee to their hometown. Inspired by countless road trips, late-night coffee runs, and the warm community vibes of cafés worldwide, we decided to create a space that feels like home to everyone. The name "Carribou" reflects our adventurous spirit and commitment to forging connections, just like the migratory caribou that travel miles to bring life and vitality to their ecosystems.

Our Objective 
Our goal is simple: to serve more than just coffee—we want to serve experiences. At Carribou, we aim to:  
- Offer ethically sourced, freshly brewed coffee of the highest quality.  
- Foster a warm, inviting atmosphere where strangers become friends.  
- Support local communities through collaborations, events, and sustainable practices.  

The Founders
Anas is the creative soul behind the shop, blending innovative ideas into every cup and curating the cozy aesthetics that define Carribou. Amine, a passionate coffee aficionado, ensures that every bean meets our standards of excellence, crafting recipes that keep customers coming back for more.
